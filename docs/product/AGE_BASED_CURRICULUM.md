# AGE_BASED_CURRICULUM.md — NAWWAR (نَوَّر) Preschool Educational Curriculum (Ages 3, 4, and 5)

> **Canonical Product & Pedagogical Reference**: **NAWWAR (نَوَّر)** is an Arabic-first, playful, immersive 3D educational universe designed specifically for preschool children aged **3, 4, and 5 years** (*عُمْرُ 3 وَ4 وَ5 سَنَوَاتٍ*). Every architectural, visual, audio, and 3D decision must serve this curriculum.

---

## 1. Educational Philosophy of NAWWAR (نَوَّر)

1. **Child-Centered Playful Wonder (*التَّعَلُّمُ بِاللَّعِبِ وَالدَّهْشَةِ*)**:
   - Young children aged 3 to 5 do not learn through abstract textbooks or dense technical diagrams. They learn through **sensory discovery, joyful repetition, warm voice narration, musical rhythm, concrete familiar objects, and playful action**.
2. **Age-Appropriate Scaffolding Without Rigid Diagnosis**:
   - The curriculum is organized around three welcoming worlds (**3 years**, **4 years**, **5 years**) that match early childhood developmental milestones:
     - **Age 3 (*عَالَمُ الاِكْتِشَافِ*)**: Sensory discovery, listening, and basic recognition.
     - **Age 4 (*عَالَمُ الاِسْتِكْشَافِ*)**: Active exploration, sorting, early phonics, and counting 1–10.
     - **Age 5 (*عَالَمُ التَّعَلُّمِ*)**: Learning consolidation, structured Arabic reading preparation (*مَسَارُ تَعَلُّمِ القِرَاءَةِ*), and school readiness.
   - These age worlds are **initial entry points, not rigid diagnoses**; a child can freely revisit earlier activities or explore ahead according to readiness.
3. **3D as a Supportive Play Environment, Never an Abstract Distraction**:
   - In NAWWAR, 3D scenes are warm, toy-like, storybook worlds (a sunny garden, a friendly farm, a colorful playroom, animated 3D Arabic letters) that **support** the learning objective rather than competing with it.
   - Not every activity requires a full 3D scene: lessons combine **3D exploration**, **2D illustrated cards**, **warm Arabic audio prompts**, **animations**, **drag-and-drop sorting**, and **simple touch/click recognition games**.
4. **Arabic-First Mother-Tongue Foundation**:
   - Arabic (`ar`, `rtl`) is the primary linguistic, cultural, and pedagogical foundation of NAWWAR, authored in clear, musical, fully vowelized (*مَشْكُول*) Modern Standard Arabic suited for young ears and early readers.

## 3. Learning Objectives by Age Group (الأَهْدَافُ التَّعْلِيمِيَّةُ حَسَبَ العُمْرِ)

### 3.1 Age 3 — عَالَمُ الاِكْتِشَافِ (3 سَنَوَاتٍ)
1. **L3-AR-01**: Listen attentively to familiar Arabic words, animal sounds, and short rhythmic greetings and identify their source.
2. **L3-CL-01**: Recognize and name the primary and basic colors (*الأَحْمَرُ، الأَزْرَقُ، الأَصْفَرُ، الأَخْضَرُ*) on everyday objects.
3. **L3-SH-01**: Visually distinguish basic 2D/3D shapes (*الدَّائِرَةُ، المُرَبَّعُ، المُثَلَّثُ*).
4. **L3-AN-01**: Identify familiar farm and domestic animals (*القِطَّةُ، الكَلْبُ، الخَرُوفُ، البَقَرَةُ، العُصْفُورُ*) by appearance and sound.
5. **L3-OB-01**: Recognize everyday home and playroom objects (*الكُرَةُ، الكُوبُ، الكِتَابُ، السَّرِيرُ، اللُّعْبَةُ*).
6. **L3-NM-01**: Perceive and count small quantities from **1 to 3** (*وَاحِدٌ، اِثْنَانِ، ثَلَاثَةٌ*) using concrete 3D toys and fruits.
7. **L3-BD-01**: Point to and name major visible body parts (*الرَّأْسُ، اليَدُ، العَيْنُ، الفَمُ، القَدَمُ*).
8. **L3-EM-01**: Recognize basic facial emotions (*سَعِيدٌ، حَزِينٌ، مُتَفَاجِئٌ، هَادِئٌ*).
9. **L3-VA-01**: Make simple one-to-one visual associations (e.g., matching a baby animal to its mother or an umbrella to rain).

### 3.2 Age 4 — عَالَمُ الاِسْتِكْشَافِ (4 سَنَوَاتٍ)
1. **L4-AR-01**: Discover the 28 Arabic letters (*أ – ي*) as friendly visual characters and recognize their isolated forms.
2. **L4-AR-02**: Associate each Arabic letter with its primary phonetic sound and an anchor illustrated word (e.g., *أ — أَرْنَبٌ، ب — بَطَّةٌ، ت — تُفَّاحَةٌ*).
3. **L4-CL-01**: Recognize secondary colors (*البُرْتُقَالِيُّ، البَنَفْسَجِيُّ، الوَرْدِيُّ، البُنِّيُّ*) and observe simple color mixing (e.g., yellow + blue = green).
4. **L4-SH-01**: Identify extended shapes (*المُسْتَطِيلُ، النَّجْمَةُ، القَلْبُ، البَيْضَاوِيُّ*) and complete simple alternating A-B-A-B visual patterns.
5. **L4-NM-01**: Count objects accurately from **1 to 10** and match each quantity to its numeral symbol (*١–١٠ / 1–10*).
6. **L4-NM-02**: Compare quantities using foundational concepts (*أَكْثَرُ مِنْ، أَقَلُّ مِنْ، يُسَاوِي، كَبِيرٌ / صَغِيرٌ، طَوِيلٌ / قَصِيرٌ*).
7. **L4-AN-01**: Group animals by their natural habitats (*المَزْرَعَةُ، الغَابَةُ، البَحْرُ، الصَّحْرَاءُ*).
8. **L4-NT-01**: Recognize the four seasons (*الرَّبِيعُ، الصَّيْفُ، الخَرِيفُ، الشِّتَاءُ*) and daily weather phenomena.
9. **L4-BD-01**: Connect the five senses (*البَصَرُ، السَّمْعُ، الشَّمُّ، التَّذَوُّقُ، اللَّمْسُ*) to their corresponding organs and experiences.
10. **L4-LG-01**: Sort and classify objects by two attributes (color and size, or shape and category).

### 3.3 Age 5 — عَالَمُ التَّعَلُّمِ (5 سَنَوَاتٍ)
1. **L5-RD-01**: Master all 28 Arabic letters in alphabetical and phonetic groups (*الإِتْقَانُ الكَامِلُ لِلحُرُوفِ الهِجَائِيَّةِ*).
2. **L5-RD-02**: Recognize and select positional Arabic letter forms (*فِي أَوَّلِ الكَلِمَةِ، فِي وَسَطِ الكَلِمَةِ، فِي آخِرِ الكَلِمَةِ*).
3. **L5-RD-03**: Read and distinguish all letters with the three short vowels (*الحَرَكَاتُ القَصِيرَةُ: الفَتْحَةُ (ـَ)، الكَسْرَةُ (ـِ)، الضَّمَّةُ (ـُ)*).
4. **L5-RD-04**: Blend letters and vowels into open and closed syllables (*بِنَاءُ المَقَاطِعِ الصَّوْتِيَّةِ*).
5. **L5-RD-05**: Decode and read simple 3-letter vocalized Arabic words (*قِرَاءَةُ الكَلِمَاتِ البَسِيطَةِ*) and match them to their 2D/3D illustrations.
6. **L5-RD-06**: Read short, meaningful 2- to 4-word vocalized sentences (*قِرَاءَةُ الجُمَلِ البَسِيطَةِ المُشَكَّلَةِ*).
7. **L5-NM-01**: Recognize numbers **1 to 20**, order them sequentially, and understand zero (*الصِّفْرُ*).
8. **L5-NM-02**: Solve concrete visual addition stories within 10 (*مَفْهُومُ الجَمْعِ البَسِيطِ*) by combining 3D object groups.
9. **L5-LG-01**: Complete 3- and 4-step chronological and logical sequences (*التَّسَلْسُلُ المَنْطِقِيُّ وَالزَّمَنِيُّ*) and spatial problem-solving puzzles.
10. **L5-NT-01**: Explore age-appropriate natural cycles (plant growth from seed to flower, day and night, water in nature, healthy habits).

## 4. Content Hierarchy & Domain Architecture (الهَيْكَلُ التَّنْظِيمِيُّ لِلمَنْهَجِ)

Every piece of educational content in NAWWAR follows a strict 7-level pedagogical hierarchy:

```text
NAWWAR (نَوَّر)
 └── 1. AgeGroup (الفئة العمرية: 3 / 4 / 5 سنوات)
      └── 2. LearningDomain (المجال التعليمي)
           └── 3. CourseCollection (مجموعة المسارات / السلسلة التعليمية)
                └── 4. Course (المسار / الدورة التعليمية)
                     └── 5. Lesson (الدرس)
                          └── 6. Activity (النشاط التفاعلي)
                               └── 7. LearningObjective (الهدف التعليمي)
```

### 5.3 AGE 5 — عَالَمُ التَّعَلُّمِ (عمر 5 سنوات)

> **Dedicated Arabic Reading Pathway (`مَسَارُ تَعَلُّمِ القِرَاءَةِ`)**: At Age 5, Arabic language instruction centers on a structured, six-course reading pathway that guides the child step by step from letter recognition to reading vocalized sentences.

```text
Age 5 (عمر 5 سنوات)
 └── اللُّغَةُ العَرَبِيَّةُ (Arabic Language)
      └── مَسَارُ تَعَلُّمِ القِرَاءَةِ (Reading Learning Pathway)
           ├── 1. اِكْتِشَافُ الحُرُوفِ (Letter Discovery & Mastery)
           ├── 2. أَشْكَالُ الحُرُوفِ (Letter Forms: Initial, Medial, Final)
           ├── 3. الحَرَكَاتُ القَصِيرَةُ (Short Vowels: الفتحة، الكسرة، الضمة)
           ├── 4. بِنَاءُ المَقَاطِعِ (Syllable Construction)
           ├── 5. قِرَاءَةُ الكَلِمَاتِ (Simple Word Reading & Picture Matching)
           └── 6. قِرَاءَةُ الجُمَلِ البَسِيطَةِ (Reading Short Vocalized Sentences)
```

## 6. Course and Lesson Progression Rules (تَدَرُّجُ الدَّوْرَاتِ وَالدُّرُوسِ)

Every course in NAWWAR adheres to a consistent pedagogical template:
- **Course Metadata**:
  - `TitleAr`, `DescriptionAr`, `TargetAge` (`3`, `4`, or `5`), `LearningDomainId`, `CourseCollectionId`, `DisplayOrder`, `DifficultyLevel` (`1 = Starter`, `2 = Guided`, `3 = Independent`), `LearningObjectives`, and `CompletionCriteria`.
- **Lesson Structure (3-Step Micro-Cycle)**:
  1. **Step 1 — Listen & Discover (*اِسْتَمِعْ وَاكْتَشِفْ*)**: Warm audio narration introduces the concept alongside a 3D scene or animated illustration.
  2. **Step 2 — Interact & Explore (*تَفَاعَلْ وَجَرِّبْ*)**: The child taps/clicks objects in the 3D scene or sorts/matches illustrated cards.
  3. **Step 3 — Playful Check (*الْتَحَدِّي المَرِحُ*)**: A gentle 2- or 3-option visual/audio activity confirms understanding with positive reinforcement (never punitive failure).

## 7. Proposed Interactive Activity Types (أَنْوَاعُ الأَنْشِطَةِ التَّفَاعُلِيَّةِ)

To separate educational content from presentation and avoid coupling lessons to a single `.glb` file, NAWWAR supports **six reusable activity engines**:

| Activity Type Code | Arabic Name | Target Ages | Description |
|---|---|---|---|
| `scene_3d_explore` | **اِسْتِكْشَافٌ فِي المَشْهَدِ ثُلَاثِيِّ الأَبْعَادِ** | 3, 4, 5 | Child taps friendly objects in a 3D scene (e.g., tapping the red apple in the 3D garden or counting 3 sheep on the farm) while hearing spoken Arabic feedback. |
| `audio_listen_pick` | **اِسْتَمِعْ وَاخْتَرِ الصُّورَةَ** | 3, 4, 5 | Plays an Arabic voice prompt or animal/letter sound; child selects the matching large illustrated card or 3D object. |
| `visual_match_sort` | **المُطَابَقَةُ وَالتَّصْنِيفُ (سَحْبٌ وَإِفْلَاتٌ / نَقْرٌ)** | 3, 4, 5 | Matches pairs (animal to habitat, letter to word illustration, color to basket) via touch-friendly tap-to-place or drag-and-drop. |
| `counting_interactive` | **العَدُّ وَالجَمْعُ التَّفَاعُلِيُّ** | 3, 4, 5 | Taps objects one by one as numbers light up and count aloud in Arabic (*وَاحِدٌ، اِثْنَانِ، ثَلَاثَةٌ...*), or combines two small groups for Age 5 addition. |
| `letter_forms_vowels` | **أَشْكَالُ الحُرُوفِ وَالحَرَكَاتُ القَصِيرَةُ** | 4, 5 | Interactive letter-form selector (*أَوَّل / وَسَط / آخِر الكَلِمَةِ*) and vowel toggle (*فَتْحَة / كَسْرَة / ضَمَّة*) with instant phonetic audio. |

## 8. Age-Appropriate 3D Interaction Concepts (تَصْمِيمُ التَّجَارِبِ ثُلَاثِيَّةِ الأَبْعَادِ لِلأَطْفَالِ)

In NAWWAR, 3D scenes use soft rounded geometry, warm pastel-and-sunlight palettes, gentle bounce animations, and locked/assisted camera angles so young children never get lost orbiting in empty space:

1. **The 3D Color & Shape Garden (*حَدِيقَةُ الأَلْوَانِ وَالأَشْكَالِ* — Ages 3 & 4)**:
   - A sunlit garden with colored flowers, fruits, and toy blocks. Tapping an object plays its Arabic color/shape name and triggers a joyful bounce animation.
2. **The Animated Friendly Farm (*المَزْرَعَةُ المَرِحَةُ* — Ages 3 & 4)**:
   - Low-poly, rounded farm animals (sheep, cow, duck, cat, bird) in a green meadow. Tapping an animal plays its sound and Arabic name.
3. **The 3D Counting Toy Table (*طَاوِلَةُ العَدِّ وَالأَلْعَابِ* — Ages 3, 4 & 5)**:
   - Interactive 3D blocks, stars, and fruits that hop into a basket one by one as the child counts from 1–3 (Age 3), 1–10 (Age 4), or adds two groups together (Age 5).
4. **3D Living Arabic Letters & Syllables (*مَسْرَحُ الحُرُوفِ وَالكَلِمَاتِ ثُلَاثِيُّ الأَبْعَادِ* — Ages 4 & 5)**:
   - Sculpted 3D Arabic letters with floating short-vowel crowns (*الفَتْحَةُ، الكَسْرَةُ، الضَّمَّةُ*) alongside a 3D toy representing the anchor word.

*(Note on existing `.glb` infrastructure: The R3F `WorldCanvas`, `useGLTF` loader, node-picking handler, `ErrorBoundary`, and `prefers-reduced-motion` support built in Phase 1–2 are 100% reusable as the rendering engine for `scene_3d_explore` activities.)*

## 9. Arabic-First Content Requirements (مُتَطَلَّبَاتُ المُحْتَوَى العَرَبِيِّ الأَوَّلِ)

1. **Full Diacritics (Tashkeel — التَّشْكِيلُ الكَامِلُ)**:
   - Every Arabic title, word, syllable, and prompt presented to children or parents must carry accurate vowel marks (*الفَتْحَةُ، الكَسْرَةُ، الضَّمَّةُ، السُّكُونُ، الشَّدَّةُ، التَّنْوِينُ*).
   - For Age 5's reading pathway (`مَسَارُ تَعَلُّمِ القِرَاءَةِ`), diacritics are highlighted visually in a distinct warm accent color so the child clearly sees the vowel above or below the letter.
2. **Audio-First Design for Pre-Readers**:
   - Because 3- and 4-year-olds cannot read instructions, every activity prompt includes an audio cue (`AudioAsset` / spoken Arabic prompt) and a prominent speaker replay button.
3. **Child-Friendly Arabic Typography**:
   - Generous font sizes, `line-height: 1.8+`, zero letter-spacing (`letter-spacing: normal`), and high contrast so vowel marks never clip or overlap.

## 10. Future French (`fr`) and English (`en`) Localization Strategy

1. **Parent & UI Chrome Localization**:
   - The application shell, navigation labels, age-world descriptions, and parent progress views support `ar` (default, `rtl`), `fr` (`ltr`), and `en` (`ltr`) through data-driven translations (`ContentTranslation` + `i18next`).
2. **Universal Domains vs. Language-Specific Literacy Domains**:
   - **Universal Cognitive Domains** (Colors, Shapes, Counting & Math, Animals, Nature & Seasons, Logic & Problem Solving): Lesson prompts and audio narration translate directly across `ar`, `fr`, and `en`.
   - **Arabic Reading & Alphabet Pathway (`مَسَارُ تَعَلُّمِ القِرَاءَةِ`)**: The target letters, short vowels (*الفَتْحَةُ، الكَسْرَةُ، الضَّمَّةُ*), syllables, and Arabic words remain in Arabic across all UI locales, while parent helper notes and instructions can be displayed in `ar`, `fr`, or `en` so non-Arabic-fluent parents can still support their child's Arabic reading journey.
